The problem

Every UAE SME just became a taxpayer — overnight.

  • Since 1 June 2023, the UAE charges 9% corporate tax on profit above AED 375k — on top of 5% VAT.
  • Even 0%-rated free-zone companies must register, keep IFRS books and file. The paperwork has no exemption.
  • Small Business Relief ends 31 Dec 2026. The grace period is closing on every SME.
  • Miss registration and it's an AED 10,000 penalty — and it accumulates.
  • Yet most expat-owned free-zone SMEs have no finance team, informal books, and can't afford a AED 2k–5k/mo bookkeeper.
"Poor bookkeeping doesn't lower your tax — it raises it."

The solution

Mizan does the bookkeeping. You just confirm.

  • Connect a bank and upload invoices — AI imports, categorises and reconciles every transaction.
  • The magic moment: ~90% auto-categorised, only ~10% flagged for a human glance.
  • Live management reporting — P&L and a real-time VAT / corporate-tax position.
  • Then it bridges to a partnered, licensed FTA tax agent who reviews and files.
