# Mizan — 60-Second Traction Script (MENA)

**Purpose:** run this as a short video (Instagram Reels / TikTok / LinkedIn / YouTube Shorts) targeting UAE & GCC founders. Every view drives the waitlist — and the waitlist is the traction proof for investors.

**Format:** 55–60 seconds · vertical 9:16 · founder-to-camera or voiceover with screen captures of the product.

---

## The Script

**[0:00–0:05] — HOOK (text on screen + voice)**

> "If you run a business in the UAE, you became a taxpayer overnight — and most founders still don't have books."

*Visual: fast cuts of Dubai/free-zone skyline, then a stack of unfiled invoices.*

**[0:05–0:15] — THE PAIN**

> "Corporate tax is here. Miss your registration and it's a **10,000 dirham** penalty. Your options? Spreadsheets you'll never keep up with… or a firm charging you a fortune to do it slowly."

*Visual: FTA deadline on screen, AED 10,000 stamped in red.*

**[0:15–0:30] — THE PRODUCT (the magic moment)**

> "This is **Mizan** — ميزان, 'the balance.' Connect your bank. Upload your invoices. The AI does the bookkeeping — categorised, reconciled, tax-ready — and you just confirm. From records to filing, on autopilot."

*Visual: screen recording — bank connects, transactions auto-categorise, dashboard goes green.*

**[0:30–0:40] — WHY NOW / WHY US**

> "Built in the UAE, for the UAE's new tax era. Not a tool that makes you do the work. Not a firm that bills you for it. Both — for the price of a team lunch."

*Visual: pricing tiers flash briefly; founder on camera.*

**[0:40–0:55] — THE CTA (this is the traction engine)**

> "We're onboarding the first 100 UAE companies now. Join the waitlist — link below — and your first tax filing is on us."

*Visual: waitlist page on phone, email being typed, 'You're on the list ✓'.*

**[0:55–0:60] — CLOSE**

> "Mizan. Your books, balanced. Your tax, filed. **قبل الموعد — before the deadline.**"

*Visual: logo + URL.*

## Why this script = traction proof

The script exists to generate **measurable demand**, not just views. Track and show investors:

| Metric | Where it comes from | What it proves |
|---|---|---|
| Landing-page visits | Ad/video link clicks | The pain resonates |
| Waitlist signups | `/api/waitlist` (Supabase table) | Real demand, real emails |
| Visit → signup conversion | Analytics (Plausible/PostHog) | Message–market fit (aim >10%) |
| Company + stage fields | Waitlist form | Qualified pipeline, not tourists |
| Cost per signup | Ad spend ÷ signups | Repeatable, fundable acquisition |

**The investor line this earns you:** *"We spent AED X on a 60-second video and put Y UAE companies on the waitlist at Z% conversion — before writing the full product. Demand is proven; we're raising to serve it."*

## MENA delivery notes

- Keep the Arabic beats (ميزان / قبل الموعد) — they signal "built here," not translated.
- Run a full-Arabic variant for Sharjah/Abu Dhabi/KSA audiences; keep the English one for Dubai free-zone expat founders (the core ICP).
- Best posting windows: Sun–Thu evenings; avoid Friday midday.
- Urgency anchor: always tie to the next real FTA registration/filing deadline in the caption.
